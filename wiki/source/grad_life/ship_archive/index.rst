=====================
SHIP Document Archive
=====================

Archived copies of the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) documents provided by the
university, organized by plan year. These are kept for reference only — always check the
`SHIP website <https://uhs.berkeley.edu/insurance-ship>`__ for the current plan details.

Click a document to open it in your browser.
