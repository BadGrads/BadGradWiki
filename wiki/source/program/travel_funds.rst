============
Travel Funds
============

There are several ways to fund travel to conferences and other professional meetings. Use your
advisor's grant funding when you can, and save the university grants below for trips your advisor
can't cover, since you can use them more at your own discretion. Once you've traveled, see
:doc:`reimbursement` for how to get paid back.

.. note::

   The programs below are current as of the 2026–27 academic year. They typically run every year,
   but eligibility and deadlines change, so check the linked pages before applying.

----

Graduate Division Conference Travel Grants
-------------------------------------------

The Graduate Division offers `Conference Travel Grants`_ for attending professional conferences.

- PhD students are eligible for **two grants per academic career** (as of 2026), so save them for
  trips you can't fund any other way.
- Priority goes to students who are presenting. You will need an acceptance notice for your talk
  or poster from the conference before your funding can be approved.

AAS Travel Grants
------------------

The American Astronomical Society offers several travel grants each year:

- `FAMOUS Travel Grants`_ help underrepresented astronomers attend AAS meetings.
- `International Travel Grants`_ cover airfare to international science meetings.
- The `Rodger Doxsey Travel Prize`_ funds graduate students to present their dissertation research
  at an AAS meeting.

Other Berkeley Resources
-------------------------

- The Sponsored Projects Office maintains a `list of subject-specific travel grants`_.
- The Graduate Assembly's `Grad Student Parent Travel Award`_ supports grad student parents who
  need to bring a child or caregiver to a conference.
