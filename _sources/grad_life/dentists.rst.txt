=======================
Dentist Recommendations
=======================

With the `SHIP medical insurance <http://www.uhs.berkeley.edu/students/insurance/index.shtml>`__ provided by the university, you have access to all the medical facilities inside the `Tang Center <http://www.uhs.berkeley.edu/>`__.  This includes GPs, specialists, the pharmacy, and the optometrist (through the School of Optometry).  See the :ref:`health insurance page <insurance>` for more details.

The SHIP insurance also provides `basic dental care <https://uhs.berkeley.edu/insurance-ship/ship-benefits/ship-dental-benefits>`__, cleanings every six months and most dental needs, including fillings, X-rays, wisdom teeth removal, etc.  This service is provided outside the Tang Center, and `the dental care website <https://mybenefits.metlife.com/>`__ provides a list of dentists that are covered by the insurance.  To help you find a good dentist, here are the names of some dentists that have been tried and liked.  (If you add a dentist here, please use the appropriate smiley face to indicate your level of satisfaction.)
